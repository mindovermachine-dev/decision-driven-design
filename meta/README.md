**Charter.** These documents are for the principal and the agents working for him. They record the programme; they are not canon. Session records follow their own convention (`meta/sessions/README.md`).

The honesty layer. Published as first-class documents, not hidden.

- **lineage-and-limits** · full attribution to prior art (Tesler, Ashby, Brooks, Meyer, Kalman, Polanyi/Collins, Rice, Edelman & Gally); the corrections and retreats forced by external adversarial review; the open falsification debts, booked openly
- **consolidated-state** · single authoritative status — what stands, what is superseded, what is owed. Read this to resolve any conflict between documents.
- **CANON-PATCH-REGISTER** · the v4.3 → v4.4 register, archived verbatim: every correction the repo owed, with the superseded text quoted alongside its replacement. Its `core/NN` citations predate the v4.4 renumbering — see its filing note.
- **way-of-working** · how work on the framework is structured: the graph model, claims, projections, the program, working conventions. Governs the repo; where it and older material disagree, it wins.
- **conversion-protocol** · the process for converting `core/` prose into claim files under `core/claims/` (process document, versioned with the way of working)
- **graph-tool-mvp** / **graph-tool-ontology** · the graph tool: MVP sketch (parked until paper 1 ships) and the claim/decision ontology (sibling on product-core)
- **seed/** · claim and decision seeds drafted from projections pinned at v4.4/v4.5 — working input for conversion, verified against live canon before anything lands in `core/`

A framework that states what it cannot support is worth more than one that overclaims. This directory
is where that commitment is kept.
