# What steps 2 to 10 add to the act map

Every one of steps 2 to 10 attaches to the act map, either to one of its parts or to a record kept beside it. None needs a part the map does not have. That is the evidence for S-1 in `proposals.md`: the act map as the index every later artefact attaches to.

**Register: `[PROPOSED]`.** Steps 2 to 10 are `[PROPOSED]` and unfiled beyond one line each in the act address session's method draft. The "Value made visible" rows below are that line's value clause, verbatim, followed by one sentence on what the practitioner sees on the map. The second sentence is Claude's, `UNVERIFIED — Emil review`.

**Weakest point.** Each attachment is read from one line of a draft nobody has ruled. A step whose content is ruled later may attach somewhere else, and its row here would then be wrong.

## Where each step attaches

The map's parts are those of `DDD-address-06` as proposed (P-1): scope, act sites, layers, dependencies and the index of determinables. Three records sit beside it: the occurrence record (step 9's), the derivation record (`DDD-address-02`'s "derivation layer") and the occupancy record (who occupies an address, kept out of the address by `DDD-ground-06`).

| Step | What it adds | Attaches to | In the map or beside it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 Anchor authority | The top authority, and the role chain beneath it | The top acts and the composite acts below them: one authority per layer | In the map, on the layers |
| 3 Surface decisions | Each decision, anchored at the act site where it is resolved | The index: a determinable taken up becomes a decision. A decision with no act site is a candidate escape | In the map, on the index and the act sites |
| 4 Declare ground | Per decision, the provenance of its ground and its tick rate | The dependencies (whose ground reading carries what) and the index's rate of change | In the map, on the dependencies and the index |
| 5 Classify closure and set assurance | Per decision, which closure senses hold and the assurance its stakes need | The index's observed state: whether a verdict there is mechanical or judgement | In the map, on the index |
| 6 Allocate | An actor or arrangement at each address; new seams where decomposing pays | The occupancy record, beside each act site. A new seam adds a composite act, which is a layer | Beside the map, and in it for the layers |
| 7 Complete accountability | Attribution, principal, authority, stake and remediation per allocation | The occupancy record | Beside the map |
| 8 Derive forward | The ground requirements each act site pulls, and the components and slices they pull | The derivation record, by an edge from each pulling act site | Beside the map |
| 9 Execute and record | Each occurrence, verdict, correction and waiver | The occurrence record, each entry against exactly one act site with its time index. It fills the index's observed state | Beside the map |
| 10 Observe and supersede | Behaviour at fixed addresses; superseded decisions | The occurrence record, read at the two grains of `DDD-address-05`. A supersession changes the index's specified state, and can open a new pull | Beside the map, and in it on the index |

Steps 6, 7, 8 and 9 attach beside the map, and steps 6 and 10 also change a part of it. No step needs an attachment the map lacks.
