# applications/

The framework denominated in a domain. Each application is a **projection** of the general principle,
not a restatement of it.

- **pdlc/** · the product lifecycle, `[PROPOSED]` (`DDD-dec-114`) — three owners: the What owner (what the product must do, everything the How needs, with product assurance), the When owner (the business: the order of all new demand and when each change reaches users), and the How owner (the SDLC, with implementation assurance and the order of owed and own-ground work). A pull system for new demand with an option pool, three kinds of demand, and responsibility placed per decision
- **sdlc/** · software delivery — the agentic/DAG design framework that was the whole of DDD through v3, now correctly positioned as one projection of the actor-general core. Within the PDLC, the SDLC is the How owner's arrangement and a composite act of the PDLC: every SDLC act's layer path begins PDLC ▸ SDLC ([`sdlc/sdlc.md`](sdlc/sdlc.md), `[PROPOSED]`, `DDD-dec-115`). The directories sit side by side, and the nesting is a relation in the act map, not a directory layout
- **organizations/** · organisation design as a directory of four claims, `DDD-org-01..04`, all projected

*Conservation of Specification Demand* is what *Conservation of Determination Demand* is called when
the actor is building software. That relationship — general principle, domain projection — is the
structure the framework prescribes for itself.
