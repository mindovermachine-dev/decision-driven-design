**Charter.** These documents are for practitioners in a domain. They apply canon; they are not canon. A domain result with no claim behind it is held, and says so.

The framework denominated in a domain. Each application is a **projection** of the general principle,
not a restatement of it.

- **sdlc/** · software delivery — the agentic/DAG design framework that was the whole of DDD through v3, now correctly positioned as one projection of the actor-general core

*Conservation of Specification Demand* is what *Conservation of Determination Demand* is called when
the actor is building software. That relationship — general principle, domain projection — is the
structure the framework prescribes for itself.
