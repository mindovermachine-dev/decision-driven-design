**Charter.** These documents are for reviewers and readers outside the programme. A paper is a projection: it states what canon held at its pin, in the venue's register. It is not canon, and a correction to it flows upward into `core/` first.

Each paper's directory holds the paper, its supplement or context file, its reviewer brief, and the response to review.
